# Brag Plan: HouseSwap

## What is this app?
A home-swap network for India where you host someone in your city, earn credit
points, and spend them on a verified home in another city — so the two trips
never have to line up.

## The angle
Every home-swap product dies on the same problem: a swap needs two people who
each want the other's city on the same dates. That almost never happens.
HouseSwap replaces the handshake with points, and the site says it in one line
of its own copy — **"You don't swap with one person. You swap with the
network."** The video states the problem in three seconds, breaks it, and then
shows the working app doing the thing.

No invented claims. Every number and line on screen is taken from the product:
the real rate table, the real trust copy, the real pilot cities.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Two home cards face each other across a double-headed arrow — the swap everyone
imagines. The arrow **snaps and greys out**, and the line lands:

> Nobody wants your city on your dates.

Seven words, a broken arrow, and the problem is stated before anyone has
decided whether to keep watching.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The broken arrow redraws itself as a **network**: one home emits points that
  travel to three homes in three different cities. The swap stops being a pair.
- **Real search results.** Three listing cards arrive one by one with the actual
  rates the formula produces — 56, 40 and 14 points a night — under the real
  category chips (1BHK · 2BHK · 3BHK · Room + kitchen).
- **Points held, not paid.** A request panel resolves `56 × 4 nights = 224
  points`, and the figure moves into a held state. This is the escrow idea in
  one number.
- **Every room photographed twice.** The check-in and check-out frames sit side
  by side with a timestamp — the product's own trust claim, shown rather than
  asserted.

## Outro / punchline
The hero line the site already leads with — **"Your home is your ticket."** —
over the mark, with the pilot cities beneath it. Quiet landing, no exclamation.

## User flow worth showing
Entry → key action → result, all three in Scene 3:
1. **Entry** — search Delhi, see homes priced in points per night.
2. **Key action** — request four nights; the cost resolves and the points are
   held rather than spent.
3. **Result** — the stay's photo record: the same room at check-in and at
   check-out, which is what releases the points.

## Tone
- Preset: `polished`
- Creative direction: a quiet, confident product film for something that
  actually works
- Interpretation: four scenes, not eight. Long holds, soft crossfades, one idea
  per scene. The restraint is the argument — a product handling someone's home
  and someone's money should not sound like a mobile game ad.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20s

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: `#FBFAF8` (warm near-white, `--page`)
- Surface: `#FFFFFF` (`--surface`)
- Accent: `#13624E` (deep jade, `--brand`)
- Accent tint: `#E8F1ED` (`--brand-tint`)
- Text: `#1B1A17` (`--ink`), secondary `#6B665E` (`--muted`)
- Highlight: `#E0952B` (amber, `--star`) — ratings only, never decoration
- Display font: Plus Jakarta Sans, 800 weight, `letter-spacing: -0.02em`
- Body font: Plus Jakarta Sans, 400/500
- Strongest visual element: the listing card — 20:17 photo, area and city, the
  rating, and `N points night` in semibold. It is the unit the whole product is
  built from.

## Share copy (draft)
Built HouseSwap: host your place in Delhi, earn points, spend them in Jaipur.
No matching dates, no cash a night, and every room photographed twice.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed with sparse, motion-matched accents
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` — the calmest
  of the five bundled tracks (110 BPM), which suits the polished tone
- Music treatment: start at 0.0s, sit low under the type, lift slightly at the
  network reveal, fade out across the final 1.5s
- Music cue guidance: preset cue file read. Tempo 109.96 BPM. Target strong
  cues at **8.74s** (first listing card), **13.11s** (points resolve to held)
  and **17.47s** (outro mark settles). Sequential listing cards use every other
  beat — 8.74, 9.83, 10.93 — so each card holds ~1.1s rather than 0.55s.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle. The jade field behind the network may
  breathe very slightly with bass. No waveform bars, no pulsing cards.
- SFX posture: sparse. Four or five cues in the whole video, each matched to a
  real movement.
- Audio-coupled moments: the arrow snapping, each listing card arriving, the
  points counter resolving, the two photo frames meeting.
- Restraint rule: no whooshes on crossfades, no riser into the outro, nothing
  that would make a trust claim sound like a sales pitch.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The problem — 3.5s
Warm `#FBFAF8` field. Two small home cards face each other, a double-headed
arrow between them. At ~1.2s the arrow **snaps** and both cards desaturate.
Line slams in and holds: **"Nobody wants your city on your dates."**
(7 words ≈ 2.1s read; held 2.3s.)
Sequential/interaction: yes — the arrow breaks on a beat, cards grey after it.
Audio intent: establish calm, then one dry break that says "this doesn't work".
Audio-coupled idea: a single dry snap on the arrow break. No whoosh.
Music: low bed, just entered.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The network — 4.5s
The broken arrow dissolves. The left card stays; three more homes appear around
it in an arc, labelled Delhi, Chandigarh, Jaipur. Points travel from the centre
card outward along thin jade lines. Line, in the product's own words:
**"You don't swap with one person. You swap with the network."**
(11 words ≈ 3.3s read; held 3.4s.)
Sequential/interaction: yes — the three city homes arrive on consecutive strong
beats, then the point-flow lines draw outward once all three are present.
Audio intent: the lift. The problem has just been solved.
Audio-coupled idea: a soft tick as each city home lands; music lifts here.
Music: bed lifts slightly.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — The app working — 8.5s
The centrepiece. One continuous surface, three beats inside it.

**3a (8.0–11.5s) — search.** A search header reads `Delhi · 5–9 Nov`. The real
category chips sit under it: `1BHK · 2BHK · 3BHK · Room + kitchen`. Three
listing cards arrive **one by one** on every other beat (8.74, 9.83, 10.93),
each with area, city, rating and rate: *Greater Kailash II, Delhi — 56 points
night*; *Sector 17, Chandigarh — 40 points night*; *Sector 35, Chandigarh — 14
points night*. All three hold on screen together afterwards.

**3b (11.5–14.0s) — the request.** The first card lifts. A panel resolves
`56 points × 4 nights` into **224 points**, and the label under it changes from
*to pay* to **held until the host replies**. The counter ticks up to 224 and
settles on the 13.11s cue.

**3c (14.0–16.5s) — the proof.** Two photo frames meet side by side, captioned
*Check-in* and *Check-out*, with a small timestamp. Line: **"Every room
photographed twice."** (4 words ≈ 1.2s read; held 1.6s.)

Sequential/interaction: yes — three cards one by one, then a counter resolving,
then two frames meeting. All three are explicit.
Audio intent: competence. The product doing its job, unhurried.
Audio-coupled idea: a light card tick per listing; a soft resolve on 224; a
quiet double-tap as the two photo frames meet.
Music: steady bed, no swell — the visuals carry this one.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Outro — 3.5s
Jade `#13624E` field. The HouseSwap mark draws, then the hero line:
**"Your home is your ticket."** Beneath it, small and muted:
*Pilot · Delhi · Chandigarh · Jaipur*. Settles on the 17.47s cue.
(5 words ≈ 1.5s read; held 2.0s.)
Sequential/interaction: mark first, then line, then the city strip.
Audio intent: land it and stop. No button, no riser.
Audio-coupled idea: one soft mark-settle tick, then music fades out.
Music: fade across the final 1.5s.

**Music mood for this video:** warm, composed, unhurried — business-calm, not upbeat-sell.
**Audio summary:** a low warm bed that lifts once at the network reveal, carries
the working-app section without drawing attention, and fades out under the final
line; four or five dry accents, each matched to an actual movement on screen.

## Privacy note
Nothing real or personal appears on screen. The member names, areas and dates
shown are the project's own fictional seed data; the developer contact details
in the site footer, the Supabase project URL and all keys are excluded by
design.
